# Auth, onboarding, and account

Auth is a trust boundary. Optimize for successful completion and recovery, not branding theater.

## Sign-in/up

- Use persistent labels, autocomplete tokens, password-manager-friendly inputs, paste support, visible validation timing, and explicit Caps Lock/show-password affordances where appropriate.
- Do not block paste, invent password rules late, or reveal whether an account exists when security policy forbids enumeration.
- MFA/passkeys/social login need fallback, recovery, timeout, and device/context clarity.
- Rate limiting and lockout copy must distinguish temporary protection from account failure.

## Onboarding

- Teach the next valuable action rather than giving a feature tour.
- Ask only for information used now; explain permission requests at the moment of need.
- Support skip/back/resume, progress truthfully, and completion without dead-end celebration screens.
- Seed/demo content must be clearly labeled and removable.

## Account lifecycle

Cover profile/security/sessions/devices/email change, deletion/export, workspace switching, invitations/expiry, permission changes, billing state, and recovery codes. Destructive actions need consequence-specific confirmation and audit/undo windows where possible.

## Verification

Test autofill, paste, password managers, IME, RTL, long emails/local parts, expired links, replayed links, interrupted onboarding, offline submission, duplicate click, assistive technology announcements, reduced motion, and legal/locale consent requirements.
