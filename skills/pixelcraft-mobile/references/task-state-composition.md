# Task-state composition on mobile

When the activity itself carries the visual identity, keep one task-bearing object recognizable across setup, active work and completion: a recording preview, a session clock, or the object being earned or built. Change the controls around that object to fit the stage rather than presenting the same ornamental card at each step. Keep remaining time, recording state or progress readable without relying on the illustration or motion.

Distinguish the next meaningful action from optional extras at completion; do not put the reward, upsell or celebration between a user and their result. For a tool with a live preview, fit the active capture controls and the current state in reach before showing the full editing toolset. Verify the state, stop or resume control, and draft outcome from one-handed use and after an interruption. Store screenshots may suggest this composition; they do not verify focus, interruption, motion or actual task behavior.
