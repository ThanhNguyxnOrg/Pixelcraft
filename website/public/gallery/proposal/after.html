03 · General Consultancy Conditions (ABR 18 Summary)

Jurisdiction: Maritime & Commercial High Court, Copenhagen

§ 3.1 Basis of Appointment & Fee Structure

Consultancy services follow General Conditions for Consulting Services in Building and Construction Works (ABR 18). Phase 03 drawdown includes € 14,400.00 (DKK 107,424.00) fixed marine timber engineering sub-consultancy.

§ 3.2 Timber Moisture & Fabrication Drawings

Atelier Nord delivers native IFC 4.3 coordination models and 1:20 shop-review sheets. CNC glulam cutting files remain contingent upon contractor verification of site quay survey tolerances (±4 mm).

§ 3.3 Indexation & Reimbursable Disbursements

Fixed fees hold firm through 31 December 2027; work extending beyond Q4 2027 adjusts by Danmarks Statistik BYG42 index. Physical wind-tunnel or hydro-models require written client authorization prior to expenditure.

§ 3.4 Intellectual Property & Insurance

By & Havn I/S receives a non-exclusive licence to construct and maintain the Nordre Toldbod Pavilion upon settlement. Professional indemnity coverage is held at DKK 25,000,000 per claim.
